**Project description**

Developed a Python/FastAPI web app that turns messy customer CSV/XLSX files into two downloadable outputs: import-ready records and a row-by-row error report. It validates required fields, email and dates; normalizes names, spaces, Brazilian phone numbers and dates; and flags duplicate emails without silently dropping rows. The fictional 18-row sample reproducibly yields 8 valid, 8 invalid and 2 duplicates. Includes a public demo, sample downloads, documented rules, upload limits and automated tests.
